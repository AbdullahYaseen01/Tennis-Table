Step 1 — Prepare your test area

Select ball type and confirm setup before starting the camera.

  • Dark background (black/grey mat or table)
  • Bright ball — yellow tennis, neon pickleball, or white golf
  • Camera on tripod, pointing down at the ball
  • Ball will be fully inside the frame (not at edges)
  • Good lighting — no heavy shadows on the ball

Step 2 — Start camera & verify detection

Place the ball still in the center guide box. Wait until status shows Tracking.
